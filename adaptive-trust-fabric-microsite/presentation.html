02 / Boundary before topology

One tenant is the default.
Not the objective.

Prefer a single Microsoft Entra tenant when it can meet all hard requirements. Introduce another control plane only for a defensible boundary outcome.

01

Start with delegated boundaries

Common collaboration, scoped administration and shared operating standards avoid unnecessary lifecycle complexity.

Do not create tenants merely to mirror an organization chart or allocate cost.

Prove the exception

  • Independent identity or recovery authority
  • Sovereign, legal or regulatory constraints
  • Safety or prolonged mission independence
  • Bounded acquisition and divestiture transitions

Separate resources do not automatically mean separate identity risk. Shared identities and privileged integrations can reconnect the boundary.

04 / Architecture fitness

A fatal constraint cannot be averaged away.

Apply the decision hierarchy in order. A good aggregate score cannot compensate for a sovereignty violation, unsafe action or circular recovery dependency.

  1. 01

    Hard constraints

    Identify non-negotiable legal, safety, authority and continuity requirements.

  2. 02

    Trust, authority and dependency graph

    Trace who can act, through which integrations, on which systems.

  3. 03

    Scenario stress tests

    Test compromise, outages, isolation, recovery and organizational change.

  4. 04

    Risk-adjusted optimization

    Compare feasible options on risk, cost, complexity and reversibility.

  5. 05

    Capability and maturity feasibility

    Adopt only what the organization can operate, observe and recover safely.

Derived architectural decision method. Evidence: STD-03, STD-05. Source catalog

06 / Mission continuity

What must work when upstream does not?

Assign a required tier to each environment. Design identity, policy, security operations and recovery to match it.

S0

Cloud-dependent

Online SaaS and cloud operations.

Central identity, policy and SOC.

S1

Transient outage

Hours of safe continuity.

Sessions, cached behavior and tested emergency access.

S2

Extended degraded

Days of reduced function.

Selected local authorization, collection and restore capability.

S3

Independent operations

Mission operation without upstream control planes.

Local authority, detection, keys and recovery.

S4

Disconnected / air-gapped

Intentional isolation.

Local identity/PKI, signed updates and offline recovery roots.

A survivability tier is an operational contract to prove, not a product SLA or a claim that disconnected features match the cloud.

Derived survivability model. Evidence: MS-18, MS-19, MS-20, STD-02. Explore tier requirements

07 / Sovereignty and safety

Location is not the whole boundary.

Data residency, cryptographic control and physical-process safety solve different problems. Evaluate each explicitly.

Data residencyProcessing location Operator and administrator accessCryptographic authority Legal jurisdictionTelemetry and recovery authority

08 / Identity and authority

Every action needs accountable authority.

Preserve the acting identity, its delegated authority and the evidence of what it did.

H

Humans

  • Phishing-resistant access for high-consequence roles
  • Least privilege and just-in-time elevation
  • Separately monitored emergency authority
W

Workloads

  • Managed identities or workload federation
  • Bounded permissions and explicit trust relationships
  • Replace long-lived stored secrets where supported
A

Agents

  • Unique identity and accountable sponsor
  • Blueprint-level guardrails and lifecycle governance
  • Least privilege, monitoring and revocation controls

Microsoft and standards guidance with derived control composition. Evidence: MS-05, MS-09, MS-10, MS-22, STD-04. Explore the control standard

09 / Cyber recovery

Recovery cannot depend on what failed.

Availability keeps services running. Cyber recovery restores trustworthy authority after compromise.

Reject circular recovery

Compromised identity↓ Vault and recovery tools↓ Requires that same identity

Build a separate recovery graph

  1. Independent roots, keys and known-good state
  2. Clean identities, devices and communications
  3. Security operations and critical applications
  4. Evidence, validation and controlled restoration of trust

Define recovery objectives and exercise the complete dependency path.

Derived recovery architecture. Evidence: MS-07, MS-08, MS-26. Read the operations and recovery position
