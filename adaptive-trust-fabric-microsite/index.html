01 / Position

The thesis

Optimize for boundaries and survivability - not tenant count.

The enterprise should use a single Microsoft Entra tenant when its hard requirements can be met, but it must be engineered to introduce, govern, recover and detach additional tenants or isolated identity domains when sovereignty, mission continuity, safety, M&A or blast-radius requirements justify them.

Governing rule

A fatal constraint cannot be averaged away. Hard constraints precede scoring, cost and convenience.

02 / Reference architecture

A fabric of interoperating authorities

The design separates policy ownership, identity authority, enforcement, telemetry and recovery. Microsoft services form the strategic connected core; local and native control planes remain authoritative where the mission requires them.
