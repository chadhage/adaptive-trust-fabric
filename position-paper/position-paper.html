Executive summary

Tenant topology, local operation and recovery requirements

Deployment constraint: Validate legal, regulatory, safety, threat-model and operating requirements before selecting a topology. Confirm product capabilities, licensing and regional availability for every required enforcement and recovery function.

Microsoft recommends a single Microsoft Entra tenant when it meets the requirements and documents cases where separate tenants provide administrative, resource, configuration or identity isolation. Select additional tenants from documented isolation requirements and assess the resulting operating costs and dependencies. [MS-02]

Zero Trust does not assign implicit trust based on network location or ownership. NIST describes the need for organization-specific deployment planning based on business functions, data, operating requirements and risk. [STD-01] [STD-03]

Use Microsoft Entra, Defender, Sentinel, Purview, Intune and Azure governance for their supported functions, with cloud-provider, tenant, workload, data and local controls where isolation, jurisdiction, safety or outage requirements need separate enforcement. [MS-11] [MS-13] [MS-16] [MS-18] [MS-19]

Evaluate mandatory constraints, authority and dependency relationships, failure scenarios, risks and costs, and operating capability in that order. Reject a design that violates a mandatory requirement before comparing its other attributes. Confirm the remaining options against organization-specific evidence before deployment. [STD-03] [STD-05] Derived Architectural Position

Tenant selection

Prefer one tenant only when no mandatory requirement remains unmet. For each additional tenant or isolated domain, document the specific unmet requirement, administration, dependencies, operating costs and separation procedure. Acquisition frequency, a regulatory label or local key management alone does not establish that requirement.

01 / Scope and evaluation criteria

Evaluate requirements, dependencies and operating costs.

This paper covers authority and operating boundaries across cloud providers, acquisitions, hybrid identity and disconnected OT sites. Identity records represent people, devices, workloads, applications, service principals, agents and organizations. Each authorization decision relates a principal to a resource, requested operation and applicable conditions.

Topology follows required isolation and operating behavior, not a universal tenant count. Microsoft services provide identity, collaboration, endpoint, information-protection, cloud-security and monitoring functions. [MS-01] [MS-12] [MS-13] [MS-16] [MS-24] A tenant, cloud or network boundary does not by itself establish the required identity isolation, jurisdiction or recovery independence. NIST describes resource-level authentication and authorization, and Microsoft documents separate tenants where a single tenant cannot meet the isolation requirements. [STD-01] [MS-02]

Compare security, resilience, sovereignty, operability, change effort, compliance, cost, complexity, recovery and safety. Additional tenants, policy layers, integrations and cryptographic domains can limit the scope of one compromise while adding configuration errors, dependencies, staffing requirements and recovery work. Microsoft describes multitenant management overhead and a Conditional Access tenant limit of 240 policies across all states. Record these limits and dependencies in the design. [MS-02] [MS-04]

Operating-capability constraint: the selected design requires sufficient staffing, skills, monitoring, incident response and tested recovery procedures. Adding control planes does not establish those capabilities.

02 / Assurance and accountability

Connect each requirement to an owner and a test.

A business obligation, a supported product capability and a deployed control have different roles. Define the required outcome, identify who can accept the residual risk, and retain evidence that the implementation meets the requirement. NIST CSF 2.0 defines risk-management outcomes while leaving implementation choices to the organization. [STD-03]

Before approval, test direct and indirect administration paths, the required outage duration and recovery access. Product documentation alone cannot establish operating readiness. [MS-02] [STD-05]

Interpret the assessment without treating it as approval

The local assessor evaluates declared inputs using fixed rules; it does not inspect a tenant or verify operational readiness. Candidate means the applicable mandatory facts are declared and no known mandatory conflict is present. Insufficient evidence means required facts are unknown or invalid. Excluded means a known mandatory conflict excludes the assessed configuration. A candidate remains a design-review option, not a deployment approval or measured risk score.

Evaluate tenant topology, identity authority, sovereignty, connectivity and recovery independently. S3 local operation does not require T3 identity topology. T4 is a disconnected operating-pattern overlay, not a command to add online tenants. A separate tenant or locally managed key does not prove identity or recovery independence. Missing staffing, monitoring, response or recovery evidence cannot be replaced by a maturity label.

03 / Reference architecture

Policy, identity, enforcement and recovery responsibilities

The reference architecture separates policy ownership, identity and key administration, policy enforcement, telemetry and audit records, and recovery access. Record the implementation and dependencies of each function, including which functions must remain available locally during an upstream outage.

Boundary classification

Classify requirements at the enterprise, sovereignty/regulatory, trust, tenant, administration, business-unit, workload and resource/data levels. Management groups, subscriptions, administrative units, workspaces, labels, network segments and tenants provide different control scopes. Microsoft recommends shallow, policy-oriented Azure management groups rather than structures that copy the organization or geography without a requirement such as data residency or sovereignty. [MS-21]

Authority graph

Model privileged integrations as directed relationships between principals and resources. A user acting through an agent, ITSM workflow, automation account and Graph permission can obtain indirect access to Entra or another control plane. Record the principal, delegator, credential, permission, target, transaction, audit record, failure behavior and revocation procedure for each relationship. Workload identity federation can replace stored secrets in supported Kubernetes, GitHub, AWS, GCP and SPIFFE/SPIRE integrations. [MS-22] Review direct and indirect privilege paths before expanding cross-boundary access, and verify revocation at each hop.

Recovery graph

Document recovery dependencies separately from normal administration. Microsoft distinguishes resilience during disruption from restoration after erroneous or malicious change, and recommends external versioned configuration baselines, retained logs, recovery time objectives (RTO), recovery point objectives (RPO) and regular drills. [MS-07] Recovery procedures need credentials and services that remain usable when the identity, DNS, PKI, endpoint-management, vault, communications or SOC service being restored is compromised or unavailable.

04 / Boundary and topology decisions

Document the requirements for each additional tenant.

Microsoft describes multitenant overhead and recommends one tenant when it meets the requirements. Separate tenants provide distinct directory roles, objects, Conditional Access, resource organization, visibility, quotas and service configuration. [MS-02] B2B and cross-tenant administration can retain shared identity dependencies. Independent identity administration requires independently governed tenant identities and removal of access paths that would restore dependence on corporate identity administration. This does not inherently require on-premises or offline identity services. [MS-02]

Additional-tenant requirement

Document the security, identity, jurisdiction, operating or recovery requirement that cannot be met with delegated administration inside the existing tenant. An organization-chart difference or cost-allocation preference does not establish that requirement.

05 / Control responsibilities

Assign controls to the systems that enforce them.

Identity and authentication

Specify local authentication requirements by identity type and survivability tier. NIST requires a phishing-resistant option at AAL2 and phishing resistance with a non-exportable private key at AAL3. [STD-04] Password hash synchronization can remove the on-premises authentication dependency for supported synchronized users when cloud authentication is configured. A synchronized PHS backup is not automatic switchover from federation or pass-through authentication; plan and test the documented cutover. It does not remove the Entra service dependency. [MS-06]

Privileged and emergency access

Protect normal, elevated, emergency and recovery paths separately. Microsoft recommends at least two cloud-only emergency accounts, phishing-resistant authentication distinct from routine administrators, exclusion from blocking or restricting Conditional Access policies, alerting on every use and validation at least every 90 days. [MS-05] Cloud-only accounts still depend on Entra and reachable cloud services; they do not bypass a full Entra outage. Record permitted operations and independent recovery dependencies.

Policy decision and enforcement

Conditional Access scope depends on the principal, target and authentication flow. Cover applicable resources, test changes in report-only mode where supported, and record scoped exclusions with compensating controls and review. Emergency accounts are not the only possible documented exclusion; service-account planning and separately targeted service-principal policies also matter. The framework's change-control requirements are derived architecture positions, not blanket Microsoft mandates. Microsoft documents a tenant limit of 240 policies in all states. [MS-04]

Agents and non-human identities

Assign a distinct identity to each separately managed logical agent, not automatically every process, replica or session. Assign an accountable sponsor and technical owner. Distinguish user on-behalf-of, agent app-only and dedicated agent-user flows; their Conditional Access targets are not interchangeable. API-key access bypasses Entra Conditional Access. Apply least privilege, blueprint and lifecycle controls, monitoring and revocation where supported. Revalidate licensing, cloud and feature availability. [MS-09] [MS-10]

Devices and OT

Intune and Entra device signals provide inputs for evaluating supported endpoint configurations and compliance. [MS-24] For devices that cannot implement required controls, record the missing controls and compensating measures, including segmentation, gateways, allow-listing and monitoring. NIST requires OT security to account for performance, reliability and physical safety; review response procedures against those requirements. [STD-02]

Data and collaboration

Purview sensitivity labels support classification, encryption and usage restrictions for supported files, email and collaboration resources. Check service-specific coverage for Teams, groups, sites, meetings, chats and third-party integrations. [MS-13] Authorization decisions should account for identity, organizational relationship, device or workload state, data classification, purpose, jurisdiction and requested action. Tenant membership alone does not establish authorization. Cross-tenant access settings separately control inbound and outbound access and acceptance of external MFA and device claims. [MS-03]

Multicloud

Defender for Cloud provides supported security-posture and workload-protection functions, and Azure Arc supports inventory and onboarding integrations. [MS-16] [MS-17] Coverage depends on the resource, region, cloud and preview status. The cited Arc multicloud connector documentation lists GCP support in preview and excludes national clouds. Retain AWS and GCP controls where required functions, latency or jurisdiction are not supported by the Microsoft integration.

Telemetry and security operations

Defender XDR and Sentinel provide XDR, SIEM and SOAR integrations. [MS-12] Sentinel workspace location and tenant association affect collection and jurisdiction; tenant-bound SaaS data requires a separate workspace for each Entra tenant, and many features operate within a workspace. [MS-11] The proposed model combines cross-workspace correlation with regional and tenant operations, plus local detection and containment for the selected S-tier.

Sovereignty requirements

Microsoft 365 provides Customer Key, external key management, Advanced Data Residency and rights-management functions. Customer Key includes an availability key, and service-encryption coverage is product-specific. [MS-14] [MS-15] Assess storage and processing location, operator access, key administration, metadata and telemetry, legal-access requirements, revocation, service dependencies and recovery authority separately. Residency and key control do not determine all of these conditions. Derived Architectural Position

07 / Operations, assurance and cyber recovery

Compare deployed configuration with documented requirements.

The proposed operating sequence is requirements - policy/code - testing - deployment - monitoring - audit records - drift detection - remediation - retesting. Conditional Access supports report-only evaluation and Graph automation; Azure management groups and policy provide configuration controls; Sentinel and Defender support detection and response; Purview provides information controls. [MS-04] [MS-21] [MS-12] [MS-13] Record each exception's owner, rationale, compensating control, expiry and validation evidence.

Maintain auditable relationships between obligations, policies, controls, enforcement points, assets, events, exceptions, remediation and accountable owners. NIST CSF 2.0 defines risk-management outcomes but leaves implementation choices to the organization. [STD-03] Use configuration exports, event records, exception decisions and test results to demonstrate whether each required control is effective.

Recovery after compromise requires additional checks beyond service-availability restoration. Microsoft Entra guidance recommends external versioned configuration baselines, supported native restore, additional backups for coverage gaps, retained logs, defined RTO/RPO and drills. [MS-07] [MS-08] The cited backup guidance recommends logical isolation, immutability, monitoring and protected administration. [MS-26] Define a recovery sequence for identity, keys, communications, security monitoring and critical applications from their recorded dependencies, with acceptance criteria for each restored function.

Use ITSM for workflow coordination, not as a dependency for recovery access or time-critical containment. Defender for Cloud can synchronize ServiceNow incident, problem, change and compliance records at a documented interval of 24 hours. [MS-23] Containment that cannot tolerate that interval needs direct SOAR or control-plane interfaces. ITSM can retain approvals, ownership and audit records. Derived Architectural Position

Native Entra recovery is bounded by the selected edition, retention, supported objects and source of authority. The checked guidance describes Standard retention up to 7 days and Premium preview up to 30 days; hard-deleted object recreation and hybrid authority have limitations. A backup feature is not proof of full tenant restoration or clean-room independence. Revalidate the current product matrix and test supported recovery paths. [MS-08]

Worked review records and executable boundaries

The local reference-validation reader provides three synthetic worked reviews: E1 single-tenant enterprise with regional requirements, E2 acquisition transition with reversible separation and E3 S3 industrial site with local authority. Each makes requirements, authority paths, recovery dependencies, control applicability and decision evidence explicit. These are review fixtures, not inspected customer environments.

Read the bounded runbooks for R1 emergency access, R2 PHS authentication cutover, R3 tenant-configuration restore and R4 S3 outage operation. Their authorization record permits local synthetic fixtures and browser checks only. Live Entra, AD, PHS and OT exercises were not performed and remain blocked pending a separately authorized lab; local fixture or process tests do not establish platform or physical safety readiness. Consult the execution ledger for exactly what ran and what did not.

08 / Complete control appendix

All 26 requirements for architecture review

MUST denotes a required control in the proposed architecture; SHOULD denotes a recommendation, and MAY permits an option. Adopt controls through accountable architecture and risk approval. Assess applicable legal and sector obligations separately; a product capability or source citation does not establish compliance.

Each record below retains the canonical core wording, normative level, provenance and source references, with an accountable role and verification method. Its detailed-guidance link opens applicability, dependencies, expected evidence, pass/fail criteria, failure behavior and exception handling. Source verification is not operational control verification.

Operational validation of these controls was not performed. Verification methods describe the evidence required before reliance; they are not reports of executed lab or production tests.

10 / Implementation sequence

Prove protected access and initial recovery before expansion.

Planning ranges do not permit deferring foundational controls. Protect privileged access, validate emergency paths and prove a bounded initial recovery path in the first phase, before adding tenants, indirect privilege or disconnected operating scope. Later phases broaden coverage and combined-failure testing; they are not the first recovery exercise.

Planning rangeWork areaActions and readiness gates
0-90 daysEstablish requirements, protect administrative access and prove a bounded initial recovery path
  • In the first weeks, record mandatory requirements, competent authorities, unknowns and boundary/dependency relationships
  • Inventory tenants, identity authorities, emergency access, agents, service principals, keys, Sentinel workspaces, and OT zones
  • Protect privileged access and establish at least two monitored phishing-resistant cloud-only emergency accounts, retaining the Entra availability dependency
  • Define RTO/RPO and native-coverage gaps; secure external versioned configuration, recovery credentials and logs without circular tenant-SSO dependencies
  • Before expanding topology, obtain separate nonproduction authorization and execute a bounded initial recovery proof with measured RTO/RPO, dependency checks and downstream authorization verification
  • Stage legacy-authentication restrictions and supported secretless workload patterns with reviewed exceptions rather than untested global blocks

Expansion gate: Expand only after mandatory requirement/authority review, protected usable emergency access and an authorized bounded initial recovery proof have acceptable retained evidence. If live authorization or prerequisites are absent, operating acceptance remains blocked; a tabletop or synthetic fixture does not replace the proof.

3-9 monthsStandardize cloud-service configuration
  • Expand phishing-resistant privileged access and protected admin devices from the verified baseline
  • Consolidate and test Conditional Access as code with separate human, supported service-principal, app-only agent and agent-user coverage
  • Implement Azure landing-zone policy hierarchy
  • Deploy Purview classification and labeling and document cross-tenant access policies
  • Define data flows, permissions, jurisdiction, direct containment and incident workflows between Defender XDR, Sentinel, Defender for Cloud and ITSM
  • Confirm license/cloud/preview applicability and fund owners, drift review and recovery before introducing each additional control plane

Expansion gate: Proceed only when baseline coverage/exclusion, change/rollback, data-flow, operating-owner and funding evidence is accepted. Report-only results do not replace enforcement tests; ITSM synchronization is not the emergency-access path.

9-18 monthsImplement regional and local operations
  • Assign S0-S4 tiers and build S2-S4 local services where required
  • Implement hierarchical SOC, local evidence retention, and multi-workspace management
  • Define repeatable acquisition onboarding and separation procedures and test them
  • Move workloads to managed identity/workload federation
  • Register separately managed logical agents with sponsors and technical owners; verify supported policy targeting and explicit grants
  • Extend the already proven recovery path to regional/local dependencies and combined-failure scenarios before claiming higher survivability

Expansion gate: For each added boundary, prove the required local identity, keys, policy, evidence, safe-state and recovery duration through separately authorized tests. Independent identity, tenant placement and disconnected connectivity are separate decisions.

18-36 monthsContinuously exercise and optimize established recovery and configuration monitoring
  • Repeat and broaden earlier authorized isolated recovery of identity, tenant configuration, AD and OT services; do not defer the first recovery proof to this phase
  • Test simultaneous control-plane and communications failures
  • Continuously link policies to evidence and drift remediation
  • Review operating costs and complexity and remove redundant controls
  • Re-evaluate topologies after regulatory, product, threat and organizational changes

Expansion gate: Retain periodic measured recovery and combined-failure evidence, close drift/exception findings and re-approve changed requirements. Continuous optimization does not imply a verified operational deployment.

The phase durations are planning ranges, not delivery estimates for a particular organization. Before adding tenants, automation, cross-cloud privileges or disconnected services, test the required ownership, monitoring, response and recovery procedures. CISA recommends assessing systems, infrastructure, personnel and processes before investment. [STD-05]

11 / Open questions and limits

Organization-specific inputs required

  • Legal and sovereignty: which laws require local storage, processing, administration, key control, support personnel or recovery authority - and which merely require documented risk controls?
  • Safety: which cyber response actions can alter physical state, and who has final operational authority during degraded communications?
  • Outage duration: how long must each environment operate without cloud, telecom, global SOC, time, DNS, PKI, update or supplier dependencies?
  • Product fit: current licensing, preview status, service limits, national-cloud support, region availability and feature parity must be revalidated during detailed design.
  • Evidence: approve a topology only after reviewing organization-specific authority graphs, data flows, transaction consequences, RTO/RPO, costs, staffing and scenario-test results.
Review outcome

Record the selected topology, requirements, dependencies, rejected alternatives and test results. Confirm the required outage behavior and recovery objectives before deployment, and repeat the review when requirements or operating conditions change.
